Questions before you switch

Everything people usually ask before moving their tools into one bundle.

Can I mix tools from different bundles?
Each bundle sets which tools are included, but you can swap any tool in your bundle for another of equal standing once a month, so your line-up can follow the work instead of a fixed list.
What happens if I go over a tool's usage limit?
You'll get a heads-up in your dashboard before you hit the ceiling. From there you can add a top-up for that tool alone, or upgrade the bundle, whichever costs less for what you need.
Can I cancel or downgrade mid-cycle?
Yes. Changes take effect at your next billing date, and you keep full access to your current bundle until then. There's no minimum term on any plan.
Is my data shared between the bundled tools?
No. Each tool keeps its own workspace and history. The bundle shares your login and billing, not your files or conversations, unless you explicitly connect two tools yourself.
Do Prism Max seats share one usage pool?
Yes, the three included seats draw from a shared usage pool that you can monitor per person from the dashboard, and you can add extra seats at a flat per-seat rate.
